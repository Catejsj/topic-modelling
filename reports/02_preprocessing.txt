[1] TOKENIZATION
    Tool: nltk.word_tokenize, the Treebank tokenizer.
    Why not str.split(): split() leaves punctuation attached, so
    'killer?' and 'killer' become two different vocabulary entries, and
    the topic model treats them as unrelated words. The Treebank
    tokenizer separates punctuation and splits clitics, so 'don't'
    becomes do + n't and is then expanded to 'do not' by the
    contraction step that runs before it.
    Why not a subword tokenizer (BPE/WordPiece): LSA and LDA need whole
    words to produce readable topics. A topic listed as '##ing, murd,
    ##er' cannot be interpreted by a human, and interpretation is the
    point of topic modelling.

    It is removed only for that channel, so 'crime' stays a content

[4] LEMMATIZATION VS STEMMING
    This run used: WordNet lemmatization
    Lemmatization is the default. Topic modelling output is read by a
    human, and the Porter stemmer produces non-words:
      murderer         stem -> murder         lemma -> murderer
      mysterious       stem -> mysteri        lemma -> mysterious
      police           stem -> polic          lemma -> police
      missing          stem -> miss           lemma -> missing
      families         stem -> famili         lemma -> family
      disappearance    stem -> disappear      lemma -> disappearance
      confession       stem -> confess        lemma -> confession
      studies          stem -> studi          lemma -> study
    A topic printed as 'murder, mysteri, polic' cannot be labelled with
    confidence in a presentation. The cost is speed, which does not
    matter at 10k short documents.
