[3] SELECTION
    c_v is the primary measure because it is the one validated against
    human ratings of topic quality (Roder et al. 2015). Perplexity is
    reported but not used to choose: it is known to fall as k rises even
    when the extra topics are not interpretable (Chang et al. 2009).
    u_mass is shown as a cross-check.

    LSA raw peak c_v      k = 60 (0.362)
    LDA raw peak mean c_v k = 60 (0.453, sd 0.005 across seeds)

    Taking the peak is the wrong rule here. On documents this short the
    c_v curve does not turn over inside the grid - it is still rising at
    k = 60 - so the peak just returns the largest k tried. The seed-to-seed
    spread (0.011 on average) is also of the same size as the gap between neighbouring k,
    so most of that rise is not a real difference. We therefore take the
    elbow of the curve: the k furthest above the straight line joining
    the first and last point.

      LSA elbow k = 20
      LDA elbow k = 20
      NMF elbow k = 15  (optional third comparison, see [2b])

    These are the candidates carried into human evaluation
    (scripts/human_eval.py), which is what actually confirms the choice.

    Note: u_mass prefers k = 5 for LDA, while c_v prefers 60. The two measures
    disagree; this is reported in the limitations rather than hidden.
