[5] LEGAL AND ETHICAL NOTES
    - The dataset is published on Kaggle under CC0 1.0, which places it
      in the public domain and permits reuse including redistribution.
    - The underlying data came from the official YouTube Data API v3,
      not from scraping, so the platform terms of service were followed.
    - Titles, view counts and channel names are public metadata that
      any visitor to the channel page can read. No private data is used.
    - Channel names identify creators, who are public figures acting in
      a professional capacity. They are kept because the analysis has to
      control for channel size. No comment authors or viewers appear in
      the data, so there is no personal data to anonymise.
    - Video descriptions contain creator contact details and sponsor
      links. They are dropped: the research question is about titles.
    - Content warning: the corpus is true crime, so titles name real
      victims and offenders. Topics are reported at the aggregate level
      and no claim is made about any individual case.
